Amendment 3 Passed. Here’s What It Means for the Polo Club.

Amendment 3 passed with about 62 percent of the vote. Here is the part that matters most for us: the new exemption is a flat dollar amount, so the saving is roughly $2,178 a year whether a home is worth $700,000 or $7 million. On a theoretical $1 million Polo Club home that is about 11.7 percent of the tax bill — against 3.8 percent on a $3 million estate. Same dollars, three times the benefit. The Author's Take · Opinion
My opinion, plainly labeled as opinion: Amendment 3 is a tailwind for Polo Club values, not a rocket. But it is a bigger tailwind here than at most of our neighbors, because a flat-dollar exemption is worth most where the bills are smallest. The savings are permanent, they grow with inflation after 2029, and they reward owners who stay — which keeps inventory inside the gates tight. We are at five homes available against eighty-seven sales this year, the fastest pace of the twenty-one communities we track. Add a December 31 residency date worth close to $7,600 to a relocating buyer, and I expect the next seven weeks to be busier than a typical holiday season.
